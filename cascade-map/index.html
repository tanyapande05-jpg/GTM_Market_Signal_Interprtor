Tool 02 · GTM Pivot Cascade Map

A pivot is never one decision.

Make the hidden system behind a GTM pivot visible before you commit. Change your segment, and your channel, message, pricing psychology, sales cycle, CAC, working capital, hiring and fundraising story all move with it. One move, and the whole business moves. This tool does not predict your pivot. It lets you watch a real cascade unfold, then map your own. Built for ventures where the product is physical and the loop is slow.

The System Layer: Delays
Fast · days–2 wks

Messaging, ad creative, pricing display, landing page copy. The visible part of a pivot.

Medium · 1–3 mo

Marketplace ranking, CAC stabilisation, A/B learning cycles. The part you wait through.

Slow · 3–6 mo

MOQ and inventory reorder cycles, certifications, hiring plus ramping a new role.

Structural · 6–18 mo

Brand trust in a new segment, review base, retail relationships, fundraising credibility.

Why pivots feel disorienting: the fast layer changes in a week, so the pivot looks done. The structural layer takes 6 to 18 months, so the pivot is not done. These are Donella Meadows' delays. Living inside that gap is what pivoting actually feels like.
